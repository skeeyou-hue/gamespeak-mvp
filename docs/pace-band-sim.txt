5. IS THE RESIDUAL GRADIENT REAL RUSHING OR AN ARTIFACT
   ratio = catch time / the player's TRUE pace. If wrong catches sit below
   all catches, the fielder really did grab, and the band is reporting it.
   fielder            all: med ratio      wrong: med     wrong <0.5x   correct-catch
                                                                         median bias
   first encounter             0.96x           0.91x              9%             +4%
   shaky                       0.98x           0.88x             10%             +6%
   competent                   0.99x           0.81x             13%             +8%
   sharp                       1.00x           0.72x             17%             +8%
   fluent                      1.00x           0.65x             22%             +8%
   "correct-catch median bias" is how much slower the median of CORRECT
   catches runs than the player's true median — the cost of building the
   pace from correct catches only, which inflates every ratio downward.

7. WHAT THE ROLLING MEDIAN IS BUILT FROM
   home-run share of conceded hits, 5-slot sentence, grace 2
   fielder             FLIGHT band  CORRECT only   ALL catches        oracle
   first encounter              8%            3%            6%            9%
   shaky                       19%            6%            7%           10%
   competent                   45%            8%            7%           13%
   sharp                       81%            9%           10%           17%
   fluent                      96%           12%            9%           22%
   SPREAD fluent/beginner         12.7x          3.4x          1.6x          2.5x
   Flat is the goal. CORRECT-only lands halfway between the band it
   replaced and the oracle; ALL-catches lands on the oracle.

8. DOES WARM-UP FIT INSIDE A HALF-INNING
   rung / fielder            slots needed  slots in half          fits?      cold hits
   Rookie / first encounter             20             9+             NO           0.66
   Single-A / shaky                    15            12+             NO           0.34
   Double-A / competent                12            15+            yes           0.14
   Triple-A / sharp                    10            18+            yes           0.05
   Major League / fluent                9            21+            yes           0.02
   "slots in half" is the floor — three sentences with no wrong catches.
   Where warm-up does not fit, the band never engages inside one inning
   and the fallback scores every hit the player gives up.

9. A STORED MEDIAN THAT IS OFF BY SOME FACTOR
   verdict disagreement against the player's true pace, and how much
   of it is HARSHER than the truth. competent fielder.
   stored median        disagree     harsher      kinder
   0.60x                     66%          0%         66%
   0.70x                     62%          0%         62%
   0.80x                     44%          0%         44%
   0.90x                     21%          0%         21%
   1.00x (correct)            0%          0%          0%
   1.10x                     20%         20%          0%
   1.25x                     46%         46%          0%
   1.50x                     75%         75%          0%
   2.00x                     84%         84%          0%
   The error is one-directional, which decides the policy. A median
   stored too FAST (below 1.00x) makes every catch look slow against the
   norm: kinder, never harsh. Too SLOW makes ordinary catches look like
   panic, and is harsh in every single disagreement.

10. HOW A WRONG STORED MEDIAN ACTUALLY DECAYS
   median error vs truth after k real catches. competent, window 20.
   stored value is 1.5x the truth — the harsh direction.
   seed depth            k=0         k=2         k=5        k=10        k=20
   1                     50%         32%         17%         13%          8%
   2                     50%         38%         26%         14%          8%
   4                     50%         50%         50%         26%          8%
   8                     50%         50%         50%         50%          8%
   20                    50%         50%         50%         50%          8%
   the floor is ~8%: a median of 20 real samples is that far off anyway.
